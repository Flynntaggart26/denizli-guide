🛂 Getting There

Çardak Airport (DNZ) 65km → bus to city. Bus: Istanbul 10h, Izmir 4h, Antalya 4h. Train: Izmir-Denizli (Göller Ekspresi).

🚌 In City

Minibus (dolmuş) everywhere, Denizli card. Taxi via BiTaksi. Pamukkale is 20km north — minibus every 15 min.

🏨 Stay

Pamukkale: thermal hotels (Karahayıt). City: Bayramyeri. Budget 800 TRY, mid 1500 TRY. Book Travertine view early.

🚨 Emergency

112 universal, 155 police. Pamukkale jandarma. Tourist police: 153 Denizli.

🗣 Useful Turkish

Merhaba (Hello), Teşekkürler (Thanks), Ne kadar? (How much?), Pamukkale nerede? 🔊 tap in phrasebook.

📅 Events

May: Denizli Rooster Festival. July: Pamukkale Culture Fest. Sep: Buldan Weaving Fest.
