🛂 Getting There

Çardak Airport (DNZ) 65km → bus to city. Bus: Istanbul 10h, Izmir 4h, Antalya 4h. Train: Izmir-Denizli. DBB Transport →

🚌 In City

Minibus (dolmuş) everywhere, Denizli card. Taxi via BiTaksi. Pamukkale 20km north — minibus every 15 min.

🏨 Stay

Pamukkale: thermal hotels (Karahayıt). City: Bayramyeri. Budget 800 TRY, mid 1500 TRY. DBB Hotels

🚨 Emergency

112 universal, 155 police. Pamukkale jandarma. Tourist police: 153 Denizli.
